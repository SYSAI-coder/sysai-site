SYSAI Privacy Policy

Operator: Guangzhou Jizhi Technology Co., Ltd. (广州极智技术有限公司)
Effective date: November 19, 2026
Application: SYSAI — Internal Commerce Analytics

This notice describes the intended handling of data when SYSAI is used by authorized business users in connection with Amazon Advertising API and related business reporting tools.

1. Information accessed

With an account holder's authorization, SYSAI may access business account identifiers, advertising profiles, campaign metadata, impressions, clicks, advertising spend, attributed sales, and other reporting metrics. Separately authorized integrations may access order, inventory and aggregate sales data. The categories actually accessed depend on enabled API permissions and features.

2. How information is used

Data is used to generate internal dashboards, summaries and reports, answer authorized business queries, and monitor operational performance. This application is designed for internal business reporting rather than selling personal information or operating a public advertising network.

3. Authentication and access

Access to Amazon-connected accounts requires the relevant account holder's authorization. Access tokens and refresh tokens are sensitive credentials and must be stored securely, with access limited to authorized operators. Users can revoke access using the applicable Amazon account or authorization controls.

4. Sharing and service providers

Business reporting data may be processed by software or AI service providers used to deliver requested analytics, subject to the organization's configuration. Where an AI model processes chat questions or reporting data, the applicable provider's terms and privacy controls also apply. SYSAI should not publish seller reports to unauthorized public channels.

5. Data retention and security

Data and derived reports should be retained only as needed for their authorized operational purposes and deleted or de-identified when no longer required, subject to legal or accounting obligations. Technical safeguards include access controls, secure credential handling and restricted operational permissions. No networked system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Rights and requests

Authorized users may request information about their data or request correction, deletion or access revocation, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. For Amazon account permissions, users may also manage authorizations within Amazon services.

7. Changes

This notice may be updated if features, data practices or applicable requirements change. The effective date will be updated on this page.

本政策说明 SYSAI 在获得相应账户持有人授权后，通过 Amazon Advertising API 及其他已授权业务接口处理业务数据的预期方式。

1. 获取的信息

经授权可能读取广告账户标识、广告 Profile、Campaign 信息、曝光、点击、广告花费、广告归因销售额和相关报表指标。其他单独授权的接口可能涉及订单、库存和销售汇总数据。实际范围取决于启用的权限和功能。

2. 使用目的

数据用于内部经营分析、广告表现评估、报表制作和回答获得授权的业务查询。该应用定位于内部运营报告，不以出售个人信息为目的。
